    How to watch this

    1. Press Play full cycle. Four callouts run through Meteor Mite's phone, The Gale's phone and Kip's console, with a caption for each step.
    2. Watch Mite's lock screen: in After, a ping can be taken without unlocking, and a miss says it doesn't count.
    3. Watch Kip's console: each card says why a responder is quiet or busy, and the ranking says who's asked next and why.
    4. Switch to Today and play it again. Mite loses points silently and drops to second; nobody is told.
    5. Want to try it yourself? Switch to Sandbox.

    Try this

    1. Press Send a callout. Meteor Mite's phone rings first (nearest).
    2. Or press Play full cycle to watch four callouts run start to finish on their own.
    3. Set the Ping wait to 60s, 90s or any custom value. It applies from the next ping.
    4. Press Skip to last 30s. In After, the phone alerts again and pulses until it's answered or runs out.
    5. Press Let it run out. The callout moves to The Gale's phone.
    6. Take it on The Gale's phone, or let that run out too: in After, it comes back to Mite as "still open".
    7. Miss three in a row on one phone. That phone asks "All good? Still free for callouts?" and Kip's card says why.
    8. Watch Next callout: who's asked first on Kip's console. In Today, Mite's third miss pushes Mite down to #2.
    9. Phones start Locked. In After, a ping arrives as a notification you can answer without unlocking. In Today you have to unlock and open the app first. Switch any phone to Unlocked to see the app.
    10. Tap On duty ▾ on either phone to take a break (15 min, 30 min, 1 hour) or go off for the rest of the day.
    11. Put both phones on a break, then send a callout. In After it passes to the nearest available responder outside Eastgate; answer for them on Kip's console.
    12. Switch between Today and After and repeat.
