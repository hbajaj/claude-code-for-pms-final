Rook Dispatch · Brief for Helen Achebe, Director of Product · 7 Nov 2026 · Owner: Himanshu Bajaj, PM for Rook Dispatch

When a responder goes quiet, nobody should have to guess why.

After release 4.2, four responders stopped getting pings, and nobody could see why: not the responders, not their handlers. This is the story of one of them, and what we'd build so it can't happen silently again.

Already fixed

Since 24 Oct the ping wait is back to 90 seconds and misses no longer count as a "no".

Still missing

Nobody can see what's happening. A number fixed the code, not the experience.

The ask

OK for Sofia to design eight changes and Wen to scope them, plus a new score for 4.3.

Chapter 2 · How it happened

A loop with no way out

4.2 cut the time to answer a ping from 90 seconds to 60. Responders still decide in about 22 seconds, so the misses weren't slow decisions. They were alerts noticed too late.

Chapter 7 · The question Wen raised years ago

Should a score recover on its own?

Wen Li left a note beside the scoring code in 2019 and nobody came back to it. Both sides of it were right.

For

A bad month shouldn't follow someone forever.

Against

Someone who has really stopped working shouldn't keep getting pinged.

Our answer: ask the responder

Slow to the phone? You recover to your own usual level. Stopped working? You tap "Going off duty". Nobody is marked down silently.
